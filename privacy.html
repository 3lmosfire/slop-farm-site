Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how The Slop Farm LLC ("we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with our missed-call text-back pilot service, which we operate on behalf of participating local service businesses ("Business Clients").

Information We Collect

When you call a phone number associated with one of our Business Clients and the call is not answered, we collect the phone number you called from in order to send you a text message related to your call.

How We Use Your Information

Your phone number is used solely to:

  • Send you a text message responding to your missed call
  • Relay your text conversation to the relevant Business Client so they can follow up with you directly

We Do Not Sell or Share Your Number

We do not sell, rent, or share your mobile phone number with third parties for marketing purposes. Your number is used only to facilitate communication between you and the business you contacted.

Message Frequency

Message frequency varies depending on your interaction with us. Typically, you will receive one initial text after a missed call, followed by any messages exchanged as part of your conversation with the business.

Message and Data Rates

Message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile carrier and plan.

Opting Out

You may opt out of receiving text messages at any time by replying STOP to any message. You may request help by replying HELP.

Data Retention

We retain message and call metadata only as long as necessary to provide this service to Business Clients, and to comply with applicable legal obligations.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at theslopfarm@gmail.com.
