Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how The Slop Farm LLC ("we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with our internal YouTube tools (the "Apps"). The Apps are used only by The Slop Farm LLC to upload videos and manage video details on YouTube channels we own or operate. They are not offered to the public.

Information We Access

When a channel owner signs in with Google and grants permission, the Apps use the YouTube Data API to access:

  • Basic YouTube channel information (such as channel name and ID)
  • The ability to upload videos and update video details (titles, descriptions, thumbnails, and similar metadata)

How We Use Information

Information accessed through Google and YouTube is used only to upload and manage videos on the channels we operate. We do not use it for advertising, and we do not sell, rent, or share it with third parties.

Storage and Security

Google sign-in credentials (access and refresh tokens) are stored securely on systems controlled by The Slop Farm LLC and are used only for the purposes described above. We do not store your Google password.

Google API Services

Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

The Apps use YouTube API Services. By using the Apps, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. For information on how Google handles data, see the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking Access

You can revoke the Apps' access to your Google account at any time from your Google Account permissions page. To request deletion of any stored data, contact us at the email below.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at theslopfarm@gmail.com.
