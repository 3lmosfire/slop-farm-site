The Slop Farm

Educational media, made simple

We make short, simple how-to videos on YouTube. Each one answers a specific question about phones, cars, apps, software, or everyday tech.

What We Make

Specific answers

We focus on the exact questions people search for that don't have a good video yet, like a setting that's hard to find or an error message.

Short and to the point

Each video is a quick slideshow with the steps on screen and a narrator walking through them. No long intros or filler.

In more languages

Our tutorials are made in English, and we're expanding into Spanish, French, and Portuguese for viewers who search in their own language.

How Our Videos Are Made

The Slop Farm uses an automated production system. Scripts, narration, and slides are created with AI-assisted tools and then put together into finished videos.

This lets us cover thousands of questions that would otherwise go unanswered. It also means a video can sometimes be inaccurate, incomplete, or out of date. If something in one of our videos is wrong, let us know and we'll look into it.
